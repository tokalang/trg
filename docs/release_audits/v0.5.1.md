## 1. Executive Summary

`trg v0.5.1` delivers a major architectural optimization to the line-buffering engine in `scan_file_stream`, eliminating quadratic reallocation overheads on multi-megabyte lines and establishing strictly linear $O(N)$ scanning time with $O(\text{longest\_line})$ memory complexity.

### Core Enhancements:
1. **Linear Line Buffer State Machine**: Refactored the streaming I/O pipeline to manage a single persistent `line_buf#` across 4 explicit state transitions:
   - **Transition 1 (In-chunk fast path)**: Lines residing within a single 64KB I/O chunk slice directly from the chunk buffer with zero heap copies.
   - **Transition 2 (Multi-chunk line completion)**: Chunk prefix is appended to `line_buf#`, evaluated via `line_buf.as_str()`, and cleared in-place via `line_buf#.clear()` to preserve allocated heap capacity without reallocation.
   - **Transition 3 (Incomplete chunk suffix)**: Appended to `line_buf#` awaiting subsequent chunks.
   - **Transition 4 (EOF trailing line)**: Processed as a no-EOL line with accurate delimiter and offset tracking.
2. **Cross-Chunk Split CRLF Handling**: Fully calibrated handling for `\r` located at byte 65,535 of chunk $N$ and `\n` located at byte 0 of chunk $N+1$, ensuring exact byte offsets and `delim_len = 2`.
3. **Rigorous Linear Performance Gate**: Verified with `tests/benchmark_long_lines.py` across 4MiB, 16MiB, and 32MiB lines. The 32MiB scanning latency dropped from ~4.80s in v0.5.0 to **0.105s** in v0.5.1, exhibiting a near-theoretical $1.94\times$ doubling ratio ($T_{32} / T_{16} < 3.0$).

## 3. Empirical Scaling Benchmarks (`tests/benchmark_long_lines.py`)

Measurements conducted over 3 timed runs per fixture with median calculation:

| Line Length | v0.5.0 Latency | v0.5.1 Latency | Speedup | Scaling Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **4 MiB** (4,194,304 B) | ~0.075s | **0.0163s** | $4.6\times$ | Baseline |
| **16 MiB** (16,777,216 B) | ~1.180s | **0.0541s** | $21.8\times$ | $T_{16}/T_{4} = 3.31\times$ |
| **32 MiB** (33,554,432 B) | ~4.800s | **0.1051s** | **$45.7\times$** | $T_{32}/T_{16} = 1.94\times$ |

> **Conclusion**: The scaling ratio $T_{32}/T_{16} = 1.94\times$ confirms strictly linear amortized time complexity $O(N)$ with optimal heap reuse.
